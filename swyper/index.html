FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Swyper upload my photos anywhere?

No. Swyper reads your library on your iPhone and all processing stays on-device. There's no account to create and nothing is sent to a server.

What happens when I double-tap a photo?

It's added to the cleanup bin, nothing more. Photos are only deleted when you open the bin and confirm. Until then you can tap any photo to keep it.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

Yes. Swyper deletes through the iOS Photos library, so deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days.

What do I get for free?

15 deletes every day, plus the full feed, shuffle, cleanup bin and storage stats. Premium removes the daily limit and adds the duplicate finder.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Swyper. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15.1 or later. Swyper also runs on Macs with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro.
