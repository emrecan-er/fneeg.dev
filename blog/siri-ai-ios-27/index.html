Siri AI in iOS 27: what it can do, and why it may not appear.

Apple's new assistant can work with information on your screen and in your apps. Before looking for it, check the beta's device, language, and region limits.

By Emrecan ErOctober 11, 20264 min read

Siri AI is one of the headline changes in iOS 27. Instead of treating each request as a separate command, Apple says the new Siri can hold a conversation, understand what is on your screen, and use personal context when you ask it to find or do something. That is more interesting than a new voice, but it also makes the availability details easy to miss.

What can you actually try?

Imagine reading an event page and asking Siri to put the date in your calendar. Apple's Siri AI announcement describes this kind of onscreen awareness, plus follow-up questions and actions across apps. You can also ask it to find details from your messages, email, or photos, such as the place a friend suggested for dinner. On iPhone, Siri mode in Camera can use what the camera sees as context.

Start with one low-stakes task: ask a question about the page you are viewing, then ask a follow-up. For anything consequential, check the answer or proposed action before relying on it. Features still vary by app and device, and Apple's examples are not a promise that every third-party app already supports every action.

Why might Siri AI be missing?

As of this guide's publication on October 11, 2026, Apple's feature availability list describes Siri AI as available in English and unavailable in mainland China and the European Union. That is a limit on Siri AI, not on ordinary Siri. Apple has announced more languages for the future, but check that live list before changing your settings just to try a beta.

Hardware matters too. According to Apple's iOS 27 release information, eligible iPhones include the iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 16 models and later, plus iPhone Air and iPhone Duo. An older iPhone can run some iOS features without supporting Siri AI. Apple also says Siri AI is unavailable for users under 13.

Quick check: confirm your iPhone model, update to iOS 27, and check Apple's live language and region list. If all three match and it still is not visible, remember this is a beta rollout.

What about personal information?

The promise of personal context is useful precisely because it can draw on things you normally keep to yourself: messages, email, and photos. Read Apple's setup guide and pay attention to the permissions and prompts on your own device. Try the feature first with material you are comfortable using. If a generated answer names a date, amount, or person, open the original message or document before acting on it.

Is it worth updating for?

If your phone is supported and the beta is available where you live, Siri AI is worth testing for small information-finding tasks. The practical win is fewer steps between seeing something and doing something with it. If your language or region is not supported, do not assume a missing button means your update failed. Keep using the tools that work for you and revisit Apple's availability page as the rollout changes.
